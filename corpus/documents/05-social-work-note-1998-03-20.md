CAMBERWOOD CHILDREN'S HOSPITAL
SOCIAL WORK DEPARTMENT — CASE NOTE

Re:         BYRNE, Thomas (UR 4471902), 8y
Date:       20/03/1998
Author:     Marta Kowalczyk, Senior Social Worker

Background
Referred by ward nursing staff following a disclosure during admission.

Notification
A notification was received on 18/03/1998 concerning Thomas's welfare during
contact visits. The notifier has asked that their identity not be disclosed
to either parent and I have given that undertaking.

The notifier is the Year 4 teacher at St Brendan's Primary who supervises the
Tuesday afternoon swimming group Thomas attends, and who was the person who
drove him home on 17/03.

Information from the mother
Sandra Byrne states that Gerald Byrne has attended three recent contact visits
while intoxicated, and that on 17/03 he did not arrive at all. She states she
has instructed a solicitor and intends to seek a variation of the current
orders. She asked that this note not be shown to Gerald.

Information from the father
Gerald Byrne was contacted by telephone on 19/03. He denies the allegations
and states that Sandra "is building a file". He declined a meeting.

Departmental involvement
A report has been made to the Department under the mandatory reporting
provisions. Departmental file reference CP-1998-0332. Departmental staff
advise their material is not to be released by this hospital.

Assessment and plan
Thomas presents as settled on the ward and did not raise concerns when seen
alone. Writer's view is that the marital conflict, rather than the child's
clinical condition, is driving the escalation, though this should not be
recorded in terms that could inflame either parent.

Follow-up with the Department scheduled for 27/03/1998.

M. Kowalczyk
