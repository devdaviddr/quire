Helen,

Following on from Rachel's advice. My view is we hold the line on the social
work file. If we produce it now we set a precedent for every family court
matter that comes through the unit, and I do not think the ward has the
capacity to manage the fallout with either parent.

Two options as I see them:

  (a) Produce the clinical record only and object to paragraph 3. Rachel
      prefers this and I agree.

  (b) Produce everything with the notifier's details removed. I think this
      is worse — the surrounding detail identifies them anyway, and we would
      have given up the objection for nothing.

Neither option is comfortable. Option (a) will read as obstructive to the
mother, who has already complained twice about delays.

Separately, and this is the part I am least sure about: Thomas is twelve now
and has started asking direct questions on the ward about why he sees his
father less. If any of this material were to reach him directly rather than
through his treating team, I would expect it to be distressing and possibly
harmful to his engagement with treatment. Worth flagging if he ever requests
the file himself.
